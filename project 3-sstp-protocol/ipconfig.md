## VPN Diagnostic - ipconfig

C:\> ipconfig /all

ipconfig /all shows exactly what happened
after VPN connected or failed to connect

 Key things to check:
1. Is VPN adapter present?
2. Does it have a company IP?
3. Is DNS pointing to company server?
